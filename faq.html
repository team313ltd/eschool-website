Questions about your school setup?

Find the essentials about what your school receives, how the Principal Portal handover works, and how to explore the demos.

Will our school receive an Admin / Principal Portal?+
Yes. We give authorized school staff access to the Principal Portal once the system has been configured and launched, then provide one-time training on how to operate it.
What exactly does the school receive?+
The Admin / Principal Portal, Student App, Parent App, Teacher App, and Teacher Portal, with access based on each user’s role.
Who manages students, classes and everyday records?+
Your authorized school team manages day-to-day records using the Principal Portal after training. We handle the initial setup and provide ongoing help when needed.
How does onboarding begin?+
After onboarding is agreed, we share a Google Form for school information. We then prepare the system, make it live, hand over access, and provide training.
Will you train our team?+
Yes. One-time handover training is part of this process. Your team can also contact us afterward when something is unclear.
Can a teacher access the Principal Portal?+
Access depends on the roles and permissions assigned by your school. Teachers use the Teacher App and Teacher Portal for their everyday teaching activities.
Do I need to download an app or scan a QR code to try the website demo?+
No. All five demonstrations run inside a normal web browser, including mobile-app previews.
Are the demo records real?+
No. Every showcased student, class, notice and fee record is fictional. Clicking through the demos does not affect real school information.
Can parents see information for more than one child?+
The Parent App can show information for the children linked to the same guardian account, subject to the school’s configuration.
Can we ask questions after the handover?+
Yes. Ongoing support is part of the service. Our team remains available for clarification and technical guidance.
