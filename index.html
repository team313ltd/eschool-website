Questions, answered

Simple for the school.
Powerful for its people.

Does the school get the admin panel?

No. The management, configuration and administrative control remain with the service provider. School users receive their respective apps and Teacher Portal access.

What if our school needs something changed?

Tell the service provider what needs updating; they carry out the applicable administrative changes.

Are these live school records?

No. The website is an illustrative product tour using fictional data. It does not contact a school database or send real messages or payments.

Do I need to install an app to try the demo?

No. Every demonstration opens directly in your browser.
