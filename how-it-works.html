Handover, explained

Who handles what?

🏫

Your school

Provides setup information through our Google Form after onboarding and assigns authorized people to use the Principal Portal.

After training, your team maintains everyday school records, classes, and school operations in its portal.

🤝

Our service team

Prepares and configures your instance, makes it live, sets up access, and runs a one-time training session.

After handover, we remain available for questions and ongoing technical guidance.

The school information form is shared during the actual onboarding process; the public demo does not collect students’ personal records.
