How payments work

Clear from day one.

Your first month is split into two equal payments. After launch, payments follow a consistent monthly schedule.

01 / BEFORE SETUP
↗

50% upfront

Pay half of the first month’s agreed invoice before setup begins. The conditions for cancellations and refunds are explained in our Terms of Service.

02 / AT DEPLOYMENT
✓

Remaining 50%

Pay the remaining half of the first month's invoice when your school system has been deployed.

▦

1st–5th of each month

Make your monthly school payment between the 1st and 5th of each following month.
